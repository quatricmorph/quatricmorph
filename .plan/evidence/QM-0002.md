### Why `apps/web/package-lock.json` had changed — and why that mattered

`QM-0006`'s directory rename** (`1cfdc9c`, on `main`). At that base the directory
on disk is `apps/web/matrix-workspace`, while the lockfile — already rewritten by
`QM-0006` — described `apps/web/quatricmorph-workspace`. The previous agent ran
`npm` there, so npm "reconciled" the lockfile to the *older* tree and deleted
every `quatricmorph-workspace` entry:

```

-      "name": "quatricmorph-quatricmorph-workspace",
