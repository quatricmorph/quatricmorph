### Three design decisions worth review

**1. The verbatim sentence is present tense about a capability that does not
exist.** Criterion 1 requires the sentence verbatim; `STATUS.md` has no
quantisation-error engine (`QUANT-001`…`QUANT-006` unbuilt, no report, no ranking,
no heat-map, no GPU execution). Resolved structurally rather than editorially: the
sentence is used unaltered, and every template carries a **mandatory** "what
exists today / what does not exist today / what it will never do" block in the
message body immediately after it. Deleting that block is flagged as the one edit
that is not allowed. Every clause of the block traces to `STATUS.md` §"What a
reader should not be surprised by" and `PRODUCT_SCOPE.md` §5.2.

**2. The sentence exists in two spellings in this repository, and an agent may not
choose.** `MASTER_PLAN.md` §3 has `quantization`; `TASK.md` §"The value-proposition
sentence" has `quantisation`. The templates use the `MASTER_PLAN` form because
`TASK.md` §"Repository Evidence" names `MASTER_PLAN.md` §3 as the source "to be
used verbatim" — a document that cites its source loses to the source it cites —
and the choice is left as a founder ratification field. `.plan/` house style
favours `quantisation` 75:28, which is why the drift happened and is not why it
should be resolved either way. **Not fixed in `MASTER_PLAN.md` or `TASK.md`:** that
is `QM-0167`'s reconciliation and outside this task's `## Program Boundary`.

**3. Criterion 8 is a constraint on the schema, not on discipline.** An `outcome`
enum that can only express progress makes "conversations that went nowhere are
logged too" unsatisfiable however honest the person filling it in is. Nine of the
twelve enum values describe something that did not work, `no-reply` is a
first-class outcome rather than an empty cell, and `bounced`/`channel-blocked` are
recorded but explicitly excluded from criterion 2's count because nobody was
reached.
