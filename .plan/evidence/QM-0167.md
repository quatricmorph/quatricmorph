## Summary

Six documents amended. The single conflict resolved throughout: **the repository
carried two different definitions of "the first MVP"** — the CesiumJS +
matrix-workspace + chat platform (`ARCHITECTURE.md` §17–§18,
`MASTER_DOCUMENT.md` §2/§20, `docs/ROADMAP.md`, `docs/PRODUCT_BRIEF.md`,
`docs/requirements/VIZ_MVP.md`) versus one out-of-core quantization-error
diagnostic (`.plan/`). The authority that settles it is the August 2026 strategy
document, whose decision is now **recorded in `ARCHITECTURE.md` §17.1** so that
rank-2 `ARCHITECTURE.md` carries it directly and `.plan/README.md` no longer needs
a precedence exception.

Method, per the task's Implementation Plan step 2: **superseded sections keep
their content and change their label.** Nothing was deleted. No phase, criterion,
or requirement ID was renumbered.

Two deliberate departures from a literal reading of the Scope table, both
recorded below and in `## Claim limits`:

1. Phases 0–6 are **re-sequenced after v1, not renumbered**. The Scope table says
   "renumber the visualization phases as following it"; acceptance criterion 1 is
   keyed to `STRATEGY_ALIGNMENT.md` §6, whose disposition for §17 is only
   "Becomes the platform release's roadmap". Renumbering would orphan every
   citation of `Phase 0` and `TILE-*` in `AGENTS.md` rule 2, `docs/`, `STATUS.md`,
   and the commit history — and `AGENTS.md` is **not** in this task's declared
   scope, so the resulting dangling reference could not be repaired here. Taken
   under the task's own `## Error Handling` clause ("leave it and note it rather
   than guessing"). Recorded in the amended text itself, not only here.
2. Three label-only edits inside files already in declared scope, beyond the
   §6 rows: `MASTER_DOCUMENT.md` §16, §17, and §18's "active track" sentence.
   Without them `MASTER_DOCUMENT.md` would contradict its own amended §2.1.
   Itemised under `## Files changed`.
